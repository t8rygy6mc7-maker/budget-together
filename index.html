Even Keel

A shared budget for the people who actually spend from it.

Need help? Write to budgetog@icloud.com and you'll get a reply from a person. There's no ticket system and no bot.

Common questions

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, and you can't. Even Keel is deliberately manual — you type in what you spend, or say it. There are no bank credentials involved anywhere in the app.

How do I share a budget with someone?

Tap the avatars at the top right of Home to open the People screen, scroll to Sharing, and tap "Invite someone". Send the invitation by Messages or Mail. They open it, the app asks which person in the budget they are, and they're in. Both of you need to be signed into iCloud, each with your own Apple Account.

Why does it say "Sharing needs iCloud"?

Sharing works through iCloud, so the phone has to be signed in. Sign in under Settings, then reopen the app. Everything else — logging spends, categories, limits, stats — works without iCloud, it just stays on that one phone and isn't backed up.

My budget isn't showing up on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple Account and have iCloud Drive turned on, and that Even Keel is switched on in the iCloud settings on each. Sync usually catches up within a few seconds of opening the app; if a device has been offline it can take a little longer.

What does marking an entry private do?

It hides that entry from the other people in your budget — they won't see the amount, the place, the note, or that it exists. It still syncs to your own devices through your own iCloud, and it's still included if you export your data.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

Open the People screen and choose "Privacy and your data". You can export the whole budget to a file and send it wherever you like, or use "Delete everything" to erase the budget on that device and in your iCloud. Deleting can't be undone.

The voice entry isn't hearing me.

It needs both microphone and speech recognition permission — check them under Settings for Even Keel. Recognition runs entirely on the iPhone, so it also needs the on-device language to be available for your region.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17.6 or later. iCloud is optional, but needed for syncing between devices and for sharing a budget with other people.
